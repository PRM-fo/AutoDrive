Computer vision

Neural networks turn camera pixels into lanes, vehicles, pedestrians and signs, with a confidence score on every box, every frame.

Sensor fusion

LiDAR (green points) measures distance, radar (orange pulses) measures speed, cameras (cyan cone) see color and signs. A Kalman filter blends them into one estimate.

Dots: camera, radar, LiDAR. White line: fused. Error: 0.00 m

Path planning

The planner scores a fan of candidate trajectories. Red ones would hit something. The bright green one is the safest, smoothest route, so the car changes lanes around the stalled vehicle.

Decision making

Rules turn the picture into actions, and each one is logged.

  • Red light ahead: plan to stop at the line
  • Pedestrian entering crosswalk: yield and hold
  • Pedestrian cleared, light green: resume
  • Route complete: cruising
